Things people usually
want to know before we start.

  • Are you a brand studio, a product studio, or both?
    Depends on what you need. We've done both — and sometimes they're the same project. The line between how something looks and how it works is thinner than most people admit. We just don't pretend it isn't.
  • We have an idea but it's still messy. Is that too early?
    Honestly, that's our favourite stage. Messy and early means nothing's been built wrong yet. We're very good at finding the signal in the noise — and we'd rather help you shape it than clean it up later.
  • Why a small studio and not a big agency?
    You'll work with us, not someone we hand it to. Big agencies sell you the seniors and deliver the juniors. We keep it small on purpose — max 3 projects at a time — so every client gets the same quality of attention.
  • How do we know the design won't look like everything else?
    Because we start with you, not a template. We spend real time understanding what makes your product different before we open Figma. The visual language comes from the work, not from what's trending on Dribbble.
  • What does actually working with you look like?
    A quick call first — no pitch, just a conversation. If it feels right, we put together a proposal with clear scope, timeline, and cost. Then we get into it together. You're looped in throughout, not just at the reveal.
  • Do you design for AI products?
    It's actually a big part of what we do. Designing for AI isn't just UI work — it's about making systems that are smart enough to be helpful without being unpredictable or confusing. We think a lot about trust, clarity, and how to make complex things feel simple.
